XLSX writes threaded comments (#67). A comment with replies or a resolved mark is written as Excel writes a thread: `xl/threadedComments/threadedCommentN.xml`, the workbook's `xl/persons/person.xml` and the legacy placeholder note beside it, with ids derived from the sheet, cell and place in the thread so output stays byte-reproducible. The `Xlsx` profile gains `review.threads`, and a thread's replies, resolved mark, dates and authors round-trip. A threaded comment is plain text, so a structural rule keeps bold, italic and the other marks out of `review.threads` (a thread's comments are now inside `review.threads` as well as `review.comments`, for `notWithin` / `onlyWithin` rules), and `narrow` unwraps them with a loss entry. `isThreadComment` is the test behind `review.threads`. The writer reports what Excel's threads can't hold as diagnostics: `xlsx.commentMetadataDropped` (initials, and the date of a comment that is no thread) and the new `xlsx.threadReshaped` (replies to replies are flattened, and replies are read back in time order). The reader reads a `done="0"` as `resolved: false` and a person with no name as no author.
